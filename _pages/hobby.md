My lifelong hobby is photography. I enjoy capturing moments with my phone, especially through astrophotography, macro photography, and street photography. I often share my photos on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/quanghuy0497/). I also enjoy origami, which has been another long-time hobby of mine.

I am also a passionate Formula One fan, particularly of [Oracle Red Bull Racing](https://www.redbullracing.com/int-en) and [Scuderia Ferrari HP](https://www.ferrari.com/en-EN/formula1). Beyond the Grand Prix itself, I am fascinated by the technical side of the sport, including aerodynamics, power units, technology development, and data analysis. 

In my free time, I enjoy reading science fiction and fantasy, including *Dune*, *Foundation*, *The Three-Body Problem*, *A Song of Ice and Fire*, and *The Lord of the Rings*. I also enjoy their film and TV adaptations, and I reread *The Godfather* every year.
